Anyone can read, anyone can post. Eight dollars a month, no application, no credential check. A freight driver's loading-dock observation can outrank a tenured analyst's desk model — because here the question is never who are you, it's does this hold up.

Bad-faith posts are quietly muted by five-contributor consensus. No pile-ons, no theater. The only standing that compounds is whether the people who read you walked away thinking differently than they arrived.
