Not a PhD hangout. A working room.

The Lobby is a slow chat room across twenty fields where intellectuals from every walk of life come to think out loud. A truck driver opens a thread; a quantum researcher replies in kind. A high-school teacher asks the question that cracks open a debate. An ex-Treasury parent reads the NPRM at nap-time and notices what counsel missed.

Eight dollars a month. Anyone can read. Anyone can post. No application, no credential check. Bad-faith posts are quietly muted by five-contributor consensus — no drama. The only reputation that compounds is the number of minds you’ve genuinely caused to change.
