From the Quantum desk · The lead brief

The Sensing Turn

Why the Department of Energy bet $1.4 billion on a field that hasn't shipped a defense product yet — and what the next three years tell us.

Three years out from the largest single allocation to a defense-adjacent quantum field, the procurement signal has not arrived. The four labs that matter are still hiring. The standards body is still arguing. And the program managers — those few who will speak on record — describe a contracting cycle that has begun to resemble the early years of CHIPS-Act semiconductors, with one decisive difference. A. Patel maps where the next twelve months will turn.
