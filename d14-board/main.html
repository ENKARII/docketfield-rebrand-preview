— how boards work

A board is a small, curated collection of things on a topic. Cards can be quotes, PDFs, photos, charts, screenshots — anything that helps somebody else see what you're seeing. Anyone can start a board, anyone can collect to an existing board (with attribution), and the best boards get followed by people who want to know when new cards are added.

— what's not here
  • · No "expert byline" requirement. Curators are who they are.
  • · No algorithm. Sort is by your choice, not ours.
  • · No "trending." Good boards stay good for a long time.
  • · No follower counts on profiles. Cards-collected counts.
— join a board

$7/month gets you collecting rights on any board, plus the ability to start your own. Curators don't get paid in money — they get the conversation that grows around the board, plus the satisfaction of seeing other people use what they curated.
