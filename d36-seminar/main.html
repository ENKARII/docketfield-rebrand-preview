Not a PhD hangout. A working room.

DocketField is the open record universities already use as the working-group backbone for the disciplines they teach. It is a slow, serious room that runs across twenty fields — where practitioners come to think out loud, and where the conversation stays on the record.

Anyone can read, anyone can post. Eight dollars a month, no application, no credential check. A freight driver's loading-dock observation can outrank a tenured analyst's desk model — because the question here is never who are you, it's does this hold up.

Bad-faith posts are quietly muted by five-contributor consensus. No pile-ons, no theater. The only standing that compounds is whether the people who read you walked away thinking differently than they arrived.
