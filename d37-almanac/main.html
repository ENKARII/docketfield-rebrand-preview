What this is

The Lobby · open record

Not a PhD hangout. A working room.

The Lobby is a slow, serious room that runs across twenty fields — the same record universities use as the working-group backbone for the disciplines they teach. Practitioners come here to think out loud, and the conversation stays on the record.

There is no credential check. Anyone can read, anyone can post, eight dollars a month. A truck driver's loading-dock observation can outrank a tenured analyst's desk model — because here the question is never who are you, it is does this hold up.

Bad-faith posts are quietly muted by five-contributor consensus. No pile-ons, no theater. The only thing that compounds is whether the people who read you walked away thinking differently.
