Three things nobody flagged in the ITAR rewrite

Jess Oduya — Former Treasury analyst · at home with three kids

I've been reading the ITAR rewrite at nap-time for two weeks. The text runs to 847 pages and the comment window closes June 17. Most of the discussion online has circled the wrong clause. Here is what I actually found.

First: the foundational-research carve-out tightened in clause 4(b), not clause 5. Every analysis I have read cites clause 5. They are citing the wrong paragraph. The operative narrowing is upstream, in 4(b), and it changes the scope considerably for university research programs with international collaborators.

Second: the comment window closes June 17. This is not widely known. I have asked six people in relevant positions in the past week and none of them had the date.

Third, and most important: university counsel offices have not noticed yet. I have spoken to three. None had read past the executive summary. This is the kind of gap that costs institutions real money and real program continuity eighteen months from now.
