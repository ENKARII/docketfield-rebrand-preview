QUANTUM DEFENSE-ADJACENT PROGRAM 2026

DOE quantum-sensing program ($1.4 B, 2024–2027)

Entry #024 · Quantum desk · author A. Patel + 3 contributors · last reviewed 2026-05-22 · 12 cited sources · 47 backlinks

The DOE quantum-sensing program is a $1.4 billion appropriation from the U.S. Department of Energy, allocated over fiscal years 2024–2027, directed at quantum-enabled sensing technologies with defense-adjacent applications. It is currently the largest single U.S. federal allocation to a quantum sub-field; see CHIPS-Act framework for comparable scale in semiconductors.

Summary #

Authorized in late 2023 and funded in the FY2024 Energy & Water appropriation, the program directs ≈78% of obligations through four DOE national labs (see § Four labs). As of 2026-Q1, no defense procurement signal has arrived; the earliest credible window is Q4 2026.

"Three years out from the largest single allocation to a defense-adjacent quantum field, the procurement signal has not arrived. The four labs that matter are still hiring." — A. Patel, brief #024, 2026-05-22

Background #

Quantum sensing applies quantum-coherence-preserving measurement techniques to extract signals at the shot-noise floor. Defense-adjacent applications include magnetic anomaly detection (see MAD-Q), inertial navigation in GPS-denied environments (see cold-atom inertial), and gravimetric mapping (see quantum gravimetry). See also [stub] biological quantum sensing.

The DOE program #

Funded in the FY2024 Energy & Water appropriation; reauthorized through FY2027 in a FY2025 amendment. Program managers reside in DOE-SC ASCR with cross-cuts to ARPA-E and NNSA.

The four labs #

  • Brookhaven National Lab — atom-trap sensing testbed (see A. Patel note · 2026-05-26)
  • Argonne National Lab — solid-state nitrogen-vacancy program
  • Oak Ridge National Lab — cryo-magnetometer prototype facility
  • Lawrence Berkeley National Lab — quantum gravimetry collaboration with USGS

Procurement signal #

As of 2026-05-22, no DOE quantum-sensing procurement contract above $5M has been let to a non-lab vendor. Three known pending solicitations (numbers withheld; see A. Patel, brief #024 § 4) are tracked to Q4-2026 / Q1-2027.
