— pitch the Almanac

Anyone can pitch a lead essay, a dispatch, a protocol, or a Things-to-Watch entry. We open pitches every Wednesday for the issue that ships two weeks later. The editors are looking for what only you can see — from where you sit — that the rest of us would have missed.

No credentials required. We expect carefulness, not credentials. Pitches are read by two editors and one contributor from a different field; acceptance is judged on what you noticed, not on who else has said it.
