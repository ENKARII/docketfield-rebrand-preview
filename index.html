Round 4 · the serious-intellectual register

Three directions for a record universities and serious minds would actually choose.

Earlier rounds missed in both directions. The first three "polished" directions were three colorways of one generic-SaaS school. The five wildcards over-corrected into magazine and zine territory — wrong for a platform built for university working groups and practitioners. This round targets the register serious intellectual products actually live in — Quanta, Distill, Anthropic, Stripe Press, Linear, Are.na. Three genuinely distinct lineages, no shared typeface, palette, or layout logic, all rendering the same loading-dock story so the differences are real.
