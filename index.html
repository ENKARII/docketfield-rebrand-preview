Twenty directions · three polished · seventeen as sketches

A rebrand exploration for a community that isn't a PhD hangout.

Twenty alternative product directions for DocketField, sorted by how seriously each one structurally honors the brief: intellectuals in all walks of life, professional or not in the field, can contribute if there's value. Three directions have been polished to production-grade craft; the rest remain as concept sketches.

Polished directions

Three worth a real look

3 directions · 6 surfaces
D11 · CHAT-LED COMMUNITY
The Lobby

Conversation threads where anyone with something to add can post. Reputation accrues from minds you've shifted, not from credentials displayed off-platform. A truck driver opens; a researcher replies in kind.

Main → Thread →
D17 · SOURCE ANNOTATION
The Margins

Source documents pinned for collaborative reading. Anyone can highlight a sentence and add what they noticed in the margin. Reading becomes a group activity; reputation builds from the annotations that helped other readers read more carefully.

Main → Library →
D20 · WIKI-MEETS-FORUM
Open Desk

Each desk is a single continuously-rewritten front page. Any contributor with value to add appends. No editor, no owner; three-contributor consensus removes off-topic adds. The edit log is permanent and visible.

Main → Contributor →
