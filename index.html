⚑ Re-anchored spirit (v2, 2026-05-27)

"This isn't a PhD hangout. It's for intellectuals in all walks of life — professional or not in that field — to contribute if there's value."

v1 mockups (D0–D10) over-encoded "credentialed expert byline + members-only paywall + 12 cites" which read as gatekept. v2 mockups (D11–D20) push the spirit into flow and structure, not just paint: a truck driver's loading-dock observation, a high-school teacher's question, a parent reading the NPRM at nap-time, a freight forwarder reading sanctions — these are the lead voices, not the marginal voices. Reputation accrues from contributions that stuck, not from credentials displayed off-platform.

My re-anchored recommendation (v2)

If the spirit really is "any walk of life can contribute if there's value," then the v1 paint variations (D0–D10) are structurally wrong no matter the visual treatment — they all assume credentialed-editor scaffolding. The interesting question becomes: which v2 flow is the spine, and which v1 paint sits on top?

Strongest spirit-aligned candidates from the new ten: D11 (Lobby) + D13 (Dispatch) + D20 (Open Desk) — all structurally make open contribution real. D17 (Margins) is a sleeper — collaborative reading of source documents is genuinely under-served and pairs beautifully with the multi-field expert community. D15 (Pile) and D19 (Switchboard) are strong second-tier. D18 (Almanac) is the warmest if "slow" is the brand promise.

Most products in this space are actually hybrids — not "pick D11" but "D11 as the spine + D20 underneath + D13 as the marketing surface + D17 as a side-mode." If you want to converge in the next pass I can sketch 2-3 hybrid combinations.
